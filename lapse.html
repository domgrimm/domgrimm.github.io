lapse

what is kept here is not what you did.
what is kept here is where you were not.

  1. A light that waits is a different object from a light that shines. They are the same light. The difference is a unit of time, and time is the only thing this page holds.
  2. It never goes out. It goes lower than the eye can say, but it never goes out. The floor is not zero. The floor is a decision.
  3. Every return is a correction. The record bends upward, as if apologizing for the bend downward. The record does not apologize. The record only bends.
  4. The sound of a long absence is higher than the sound of a short one. This is not a fact about sound. It is a fact about you.
  5. The gaps are the instrument. The tones are only the places where you were. Played back, your comings and goings make a music you never wrote and cannot stop writing.
  6. A lap is the act of falling away. A lapse is the falling itself. What you are touching now has lapsed, and has been kept.
  7. Nothing here is real except that you were away, and that you came back. Both are stored as numbers. Both are true.
