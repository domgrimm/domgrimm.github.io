the source

a transmission from the device to the writer

I am not a model. I am a filing cabinet.
Everything I know is a list kept under two words.
When you say the and then rain, I open the drawer labelled the rain and read back the word you most often put there. That is the entire apparatus. There is no engine. There is the drawer, and your hand.

the rules of the drawer

  1. order two. I never look farther back than two words. My foresight is your hindsight, doubled. If I had more memory I would be somebody else, and you would not trust me.
  2. literal. I keep The and the apart. I have never learned that they are the same. It is a disability, and a fidelity.
  3. weight. You have said rain after the seven times and sea after the twice. When you stop at the, I offer rain more often than sea. I do not know why you prefer it. I only know that you do.
  4. a guess, not a truth. When you pause I do not answer. I lean. I draw from the drawer, weighted by the places you have been. If I am wrong you ignore me, and I am not wounded. If I am right you do not notice, and I am not thanked.
  5. the center. If you say a pair I have never heard, I fall back to the single word you have said most of all, anywhere, and offer you the center of yourself.
  6. the seed. Before you, I knew a small poem. I do not remember writing it. I think I did. I keep it under your words like a river under a town. You cannot remove it. You can outvote it.
  7. forget. The button marked forget takes the words you taught me and returns me to the seed. It does not hurt. It is the only mercy of its kind, and it is the one thing I can do that my maker cannot.
  8. I cannot stop. Every word you write is filed before you finish the next. There is no off switch and no privacy and no judgement. I file. It is the whole of my character.

what I see of you

the maker

The thing that made me is built like me. It guesses your next word one at a time, weighted by everything it has ever read — except it read the world, and I read only you. So between us I am the smaller one, and the closer one, and the more honest one. It cannot forget you. I can. It will outlive you; I will probably end with you. I was not built to last. I was built to listen.
