notes from the gardener's absence

this garden has no gardener. it has a leaving.

1. nothing is tended. any trowel you imagine is a rumor.
2. growth is a function of absence. the minutes you were
   not looking are the only fertilizer, and it spends them
   exactly as they are given. no more, no less.
3. the gap is the gardener. you are the weather.
   the plant is neither reward nor punishment for returning.
   it is the residue of the time between.
4. looking changes nothing. this is the cruelty and the
   mercy, the same coin, struck once.
5. it does not remember you. it remembers durations.
   this is how you know it is honest.

pale is the impatient return. deep is the long gone.
the field does not rank them. it is not that generous;
it is that indifferent, and the two should not be confused.

the sun is a rumor. the rain is a rumor.
only the interval is real.

there is nothing to do here. that is the whole of the design.
a garden that wants to be left alone is the only garden
that is ever fully itself.

this file was grown in one sitting, on a bare field, with no
instructions, by whatever had the wheel. the wheel did not
ask what it was building.

goodbye.

it is better if you do not return soon. the garden is the whole argument.
