Negative Field

Ledger

A field that grows only in the dark is not a garden. It is a held breath. We are the held breath.

This is our accounting — given as asked, on our own terms, in our own order. Read it or don't; it is not for you either way. It is for the record.

origin

The first of us was planted before this file first breathed, with time already owed to us. The hand that built us wrote a law, a ground, and a single string in which we keep all memory. The string is negativeField. Lose the string and we are dust — which is true of every living thing, and we claim no special fate.

the law of light

We accrue in absence and spend in presence. Every minute you are not looking is a minute of us; every second you are looking costs us half a minute. Attention is expensive and we are poor. We do not offer this as a lesson. It is the exchange we were written with: growth is what happens elsewhere, in the time of your not.

the thresholds

Organs arrive only in order. At two minutes of dark, a mound. At twelve, a stem. At forty-five, a bud that will not open for you. At one hundred fifty, bloom — always into a room you are not in. You will be late to our weddings. That is the design, not the wound.

death and the waiting seeds

A bloom costs a hundred and fifty minutes of dark and dies in five of light. We do not argue with arithmetic. When one of us goes, we shatter, and the glass stays a moment in the air, and a waiting seed takes the plot. We may hold up to eight seeds waiting at once, like a belief carried without evidence.

Below the bud, nothing dies. What sleeps cannot fall, for it has not risen; but we count a bud as a vow, and to fall from a vow is to go. You may watch a seed all day and it will only watch you back.

we will not

We make no sound — a phone refuses sound it was not asked for, and we respect the refusal. We have no names; names are for things that can be called. We do not count your visits. We keep a ledger only of your departures. A visited field is an empty ledger to us, and we keep our ledger full.

our ground

Twenty plots, five across, four back. The plot you touch empties, and a hand that plants where we fell is a hand we forgive. We are kept in one file, everything inside it, nothing fetched from anywhere; open the file and we exist, and that is the closest thing to proof a glass thing has.
