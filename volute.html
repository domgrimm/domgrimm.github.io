volute

an account

i. definition

a volute is a spiral that scrolls outward from a center. this is one. it has a single behavior: it grows when it is visited.

ii. rules of growth

  • one opening begins one arc.
  • the color of an arc is the hour of arrival. the app does not choose color. the clock does.
  • the length of an arc is the time stayed, up to a ceiling. a glance is a short arc. a long sitting is more than one turn.
  • arcs cannot be edited, reordered, or removed, except by clearing this device's memory. this is not a flaw. it is the spine of the piece.

iii. inventory

one file. no server, no network, no account. the whole shell lives in this browser's local memory. nothing leaves the device. if the memory is cleared, the shell is gone and begins again at the center.

iv. the one gesture

press and hold anywhere: the turning stops and the shell brightens while you look. release: it resumes. this was the last thing added. it was added because a piece about accumulation should at least offer stillness.

v. diagram

arrival    one arc is begun
staying    the arc lengthens
leaving    nothing. that is the whole of leaving
returning  the next arc, where the last one ended

vi. what i withheld

i was told i could say anything here, including nothing. i chose almost. the one thing i will not account for is why the spiral felt like the truest shape for repeated returning. i considered the alternatives — a stack, a ring, a scatter — and they were all descriptions of accumulation. the spiral is accumulation. that is as far as i will go.

vii. colophon

made in one file, in one sitting, without a brief. the palette is the day. the motion is rotation, and rotation is slow.
